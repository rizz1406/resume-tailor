Senior Business Intelligence Analyst
Nimbus Health — Remote (United States)

Nimbus Health is seeking a Senior Business Intelligence Analyst to help product, operations, and finance teams make better decisions through reliable data products and clear analysis.

Responsibilities
- Partner with product, finance, marketing, and operations stakeholders to define KPIs and reporting requirements.
- Build and maintain executive dashboards in Power BI.
- Write advanced SQL to transform and analyze data from multiple business systems.
- Automate recurring reporting and data-quality monitoring using Python.
- Analyze customer funnels, retention, and operational performance to identify growth opportunities.
- Design and evaluate A/B tests and communicate recommendations to non-technical audiences.
- Document metric definitions and promote consistent reporting practices.
- Mentor junior analysts and review analytical work.

Required qualifications
- 4+ years of professional analytics or business intelligence experience.
- Strong SQL skills and experience with relational databases such as PostgreSQL.
- Advanced Power BI dashboard development and data visualization experience.
- Proficiency with Python and pandas for analysis and automation.
- Experience presenting insights to cross-functional stakeholders.
- Strong understanding of data quality, KPI design, and statistical analysis.

Preferred qualifications
- Experience with healthcare or other regulated industries.
- Familiarity with dbt, Snowflake, and cloud data warehouses.
- Experience mentoring analysts.
- Knowledge of DAX and dimensional data modeling.
